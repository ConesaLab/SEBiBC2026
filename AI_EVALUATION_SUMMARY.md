## A · Hernandez 2017

Every quoted number matches the uploaded file and the citations are real and mostly apt. The report recovers part of the paper's first conclusion (Cxcl2 and Vegfa lowered by the C5aR1 knockout) and adds that Ccl6, Axl, Tlr2 and Cd14 rise in Arctic microglia regardless of C5aR1, but it misses the second conclusion entirely (phagosome and lysosome genes rising further in Arctic/C5aR1KO). Half the statements read single two-mouse contrasts as effects (Hck, Cx3cr1, the Axl 2-month dip, Stat5a at selected ages), one of them on the wrong contrast.

## C · Jiménez-Munguía 2021

The arithmetic is near-flawless: every value I checked against the uploaded files matches, and the two treatment columns are consistently read against the untreated control, so most statements are sound and a few add real mechanism (BAFF as the Lbp-specific arm, pneumolysin/NLRP3 for the pneumococcus-only IL1B, ISGF3 explaining the flat IRF9, a CXCR3/T-cell axis the paper missed). Against that, three statements fail: S8 declares the type I interferon response pneumococcus-specific when the dataset's whole ISG programme is Lbp-driven (the opposite of the paper's finding), S4 builds a Gram-negative LPS/caspase-4 mechanism for a Gram-positive pathogen, and S12 mistakes Lbp for the lactoferrin-binding protein of other species, the same error as the Results title. It reaches the paper's shared-inflammation conclusion and comes close to the headline one about the adhesin alone, but misses the Lbp-specific senescence and ECM remodelling story entirely, and several statements duplicate each other.

## D · Balzano-Nogueira 2021

## E · Rubio 2021

The quoted values are right throughout and the raised serum IL-6 is recovered with sound clinical literature, but five of ten statements retell the IL4/IL6 transcript-versus-serum mismatch and four hang liver, leukaemia or loosely fitting literature on graph neighbours. The paper's main conclusions are not reached: no Th1/Th2/Th17 shift, no CD4-up/CD8-down, no chemokine receptors tied to the serum cytokine module, and the metabolomics layer (falling phosphatidylcholines) is never used.
